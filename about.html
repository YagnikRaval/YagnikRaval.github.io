About

I write short poems and essays that look at memory, place, and small domestic wonders. This site hosts experiments and short pieces.

How to read and contribute

  1. Read posts — click any demo post from the Home page.
  2. To add posts: in a Jekyll site you'd add files in _posts/. For now, use standalone HTML files in /posts/.
  3. Contact me via the contact page to suggest collaborations.
