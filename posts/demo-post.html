A Small Note on Nightingales

August 2, 2026 · Reading: 2 min

They sing between trains — an idea arriving in the hollow of a station, a thin bright note that is not for the maps. I wrote down the way the light kept lifting at the platform edge. The poem is small enough to fit in a pocket and large enough to keep the night awake.

The syllables move like coins in a bowl: silver, round, a tiny music you forget until you need it again.

— Yagnik
